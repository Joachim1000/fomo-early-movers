Methodology and limitations

Each first-alert record is treated as a hypothetical entry at its recorded alert price, not an executable fill. Exit uses the recorded 5m or 10m checkpoint price and its actual observation timestamp; delayed measurements are not treated as exact 5m/10m executions. Estimated round-trip costs are deducted from gross returns. Results are not proof of achievable returns: no bid/ask, liquidity impact, token sellability, priority fees, latency or failed transactions are simulated. Missing exit prices are excluded from P&L, not counted as wins or losses. The daily trade cap selects earliest signals by UTC timestamp. No stop-loss or take-profit is assumed between sparse checkpoints.

For true forward paper trading, the collector must persist every new signal and subsequent price observation into an append-only trade ledger. This page currently replays the available historical observations.
