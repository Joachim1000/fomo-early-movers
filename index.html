Observation Criteria & Methodology

Data sources

  • DEX Screener public API: token prices, liquidity, trading volume and transaction activity in the latest data snapshot.
  • FOMO Trending: included by the upstream collection when available. The source JSON reports whether the FOMO Trending capture succeeded, when it was captured and whether it was marked stale.
  • GitHub data files: latest.json contains the current snapshot; history.json contains recorded observations.
  • FOMO app tradability, contract safety and executable prices are not independently verified by this website.

Observation & update frequency

  • 5-minute metrics: buy/sell transaction counts and volume refer to a rolling 5-minute market window. This does not mean the dashboard collects data every 5 minutes.
  • Historical observation window: the TOP 10 source field is eligible_4h_candidates. The chartless trend comparison uses available matching-mint, matching-pair observations; the actual sampling interval and coverage depend on the stored history.
  • Website maintenance: scheduled hourly at minute :05. This is a website/data check, not proof of a new market-data capture.
  • Dashboard refresh: fetches the latest committed JSON files whenever the page is opened or the Refresh button is clicked. There is no automatic live streaming.
  • Upstream data collection cadence: not independently established by the website. Refer to snapshot and capture timestamps below rather than assuming a fixed 5- or 15-minute collection interval.

FOMO TRADING · QUICK GUIDE

When to buy and sell using candlesticks, RSI, MACD (blue & red), and volume. English adaptation of the supplied one-page illustrated guide.

Candlesticks · Basics

Green candle: price closes above its open. Red candle: price closes below its open.

Body = open-to-close range. Upper wick = highest price. Lower wick = lowest price.

Key patterns

Hammer, inverted hammer, doji, bullish/bearish engulfing, morning star, evening star, higher highs (uptrend), lower lows (downtrend).

Recommended chart settings

  • Primary timeframe: 5 minutes
  • Confirm trend: 15 minutes
  • RSI: 14, levels 30 / 50 / 70
  • MACD: 8 / 21 / 5 (blue & red)
  • Volume: enabled
  • Timezone: UTC recommended

RSI (14) · Momentum

Above 70: overbought; watch for weakness.

Around 50–70: stronger momentum; often a favorable area in the source guide.

Below 30: oversold; possible reversal, not a guarantee.

MACD (8/21/5) · Blue & Red

Blue: MACD line (8 EMA − 21 EMA).

Red: signal line (5 EMA of MACD).

Bullish crossover: blue crosses above red.

Bearish crossover: blue crosses below red.

Volume · Confirmation

Rising volume can confirm a price move. Falling volume may indicate weakening momentum.

Example trade sequence

1 · Buy: MACD blue crosses above red, RSI > 50, volume rises, price breaks resistance.

2 · Hold: price keeps rising, MACD lines separate, volume stays elevated.

3 · Sell: RSI > 70, MACD blue starts turning down, volume falls, bearish candle appears.

BUY SIGNAL · CHECKLIST

  • MACD blue crosses above red
  • RSI rising through 50 (preferably 50–65)
  • Increasing volume
  • Price breaks resistance / forms higher highs
  • Not already an extreme pump (+5–20% or more)
  • Trend confirmed on 15m, not sharply falling

SELL SIGNAL · CHECKLIST

  • RSI above 70 (overbought)
  • MACD blue turns down or crosses red
  • Declining volume
  • Signs of weakness, e.g. long upper wicks or bearish engulfing
  • Set stop-loss / take partial profits after +20–50%
  • Sell all on clear trend reversal

Strategy examples

Scalp 5-minute chart; aim around +10–30%, exit on weakness.

Swing 5–15-minute chart; aim around +30–100%, exit when RSI reaches 70–80.

Trend 15–60-minute chart; use trailing stop and sell on MACD bearish cross.

Risk & Tips

  • Always use a stop-loss
  • Take profits in stages
  • Avoid buying after an extended pump
  • Check liquidity and volume
  • Watch for false breakouts
  • Confirm on 15-minute chart
  • Trade only money you can afford to lose
  • Crypto is extremely volatile
