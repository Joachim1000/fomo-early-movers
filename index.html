Observation Criteria & Methodology

Data sources & refresh

  • The dashboard reads the latest committed data/latest.json and data/history.json from GitHub when opened or refreshed.
  • The hourly maintenance check is separate from data collection; the dashboard does not independently stream live trades.
  • The displayed snapshot time is the source timestamp, not a guarantee of live market prices.

TOP 10 candidate selection

  • Display up to 10 entries from the source field eligible_4h_candidates, in the order provided by the data source.
  • Exclude the wrapped SOL mint (So111…1112).
  • Candidate eligibility is determined upstream; the website does not independently confirm that a token can be found or traded in the official FOMO app.

Price and trend observations

  • Use the exact token mint and matching pair address when reading recorded historical prices.
  • Observed change compares the first and latest valid recorded prices for that pair; it is not necessarily a continuous four-hour trend.
  • Show N/A when there are fewer than two valid observations. Never invent missing candles, highs, lows or returns.
  • Liquidity, price, 5-minute volume and 5-minute buy/sell transaction counts come from the latest data snapshot.

Signal interpretation

  • Colored token dots indicate only the 5-minute buy-versus-sell transaction balance, not an investment recommendation.
  • WAIT means there is no independently verified buy signal. A positive observed change does not imply a future uptrend.
  • Previous Recommendations may be graded GOOD, MIXED, BAD or NOT VERIFIABLE only when a timestamped earlier call and independently verified later prices exist. Historical change alone is not realized profit.
  • No trader-profile analysis is performed and no emails are sent.

Screening information only. Cryptocurrency trading carries significant risk. Official FOMO app availability, contract safety and executable prices are not independently verified.
